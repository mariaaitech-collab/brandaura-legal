Privacy Policy

A clear overview of how BrandAura handles information when you use the Service.

Effective date: September 8, 2026

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how Meveryx (“Meveryx,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you use BrandAura, our social media scheduling and publishing tool, and related websites and services (collectively, the “Service”).

2. Information We Collect

  • Account information: name, email address, password or authentication details, and workspace information.
  • Content and scheduling data: media, captions, drafts, publishing instructions, campaign details, and calendar information you provide.
  • Connected account data: profile identifiers, page or organization details, access tokens, permissions, and publishing responses from platforms such as Meta, LinkedIn, and TikTok, as authorized by you.
  • Usage and device data: IP address, browser and device type, approximate location derived from IP, log events, and interactions with the Service.
  • Support communications: information you provide when you contact us.

3. How We Use Information

We use information to provide and secure BrandAura; connect and manage your requested Meta, LinkedIn, and TikTok integrations; schedule and publish content; maintain backups and service records; respond to support requests; understand usage and improve features; prevent fraud and abuse; comply with legal obligations; and communicate important account or service updates.

4. API Integrations

When you authorize an integration, BrandAura accesses only the information and permissions needed for the features you request. We use data received through Meta, LinkedIn, and TikTok APIs to operate those features, such as preparing, scheduling, and publishing your content. We do not sell connected-platform data or use it for unrelated advertising. You can revoke an integration through BrandAura or the relevant platform, though a platform may take time to process the change.

5. Sharing

We may share information with service providers that host, secure, analyze, or support BrandAura under appropriate confidentiality obligations; with social platforms when you direct us to use an integration; with professional advisers; or when required by law, legal process, or a good-faith need to protect rights, safety, and the Service. We do not sell your personal information.

6. Retention and Security

We retain information for as long as needed to provide the Service, fulfill the purposes described here, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and meet legal requirements. We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Choices and Rights

You may review or update account information, disconnect social accounts, delete content, or request access, correction, or deletion of personal information, subject to applicable law and legitimate retention needs. You may also opt out of non-essential marketing communications. Contact us to make a request; we may need to verify your identity first.

8. Children’s Privacy

BrandAura is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information, please contact us.

9. International Processing

Your information may be processed in countries other than where you live. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for cross-border transfers.

10. Changes and Contact

We may update this Policy as BrandAura evolves. We will post the updated version and effective date on this page. For privacy questions or requests, email privacy@brandaura.app.
